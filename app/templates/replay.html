04How to read it
  • Old scoring: every feature compared to the person's median, and every session, flagged or not, joins their baseline. In a long slowdown the baseline learns the problem and the alerts fade.
  • Current scoring: pauses are judged against the person's own pause pattern, and flagged sessions stay out of the baseline until a human accepts them.
  • New equipment: the person becomes slower for good (gloves, a different device). The tool flags it so a human looks, the human accepts it as their new normal, and a later real slowdown is judged against that. Size, timing and the later slowdown are illustrative assumptions.
  • One simulated person proves nothing. Try several random people. The 300-person results, with their limits, are on the Evidence page.
  • This simulates changes in interaction timing. It says nothing about fatigue, illness or fitness for duty.
